Latent state (z_t)
The working latent — a single slot the loop reads and rewrites each step. It is seeded once from the initial noise (z_T), overwritten every step by the scheduler's output (z_{t-1}), and read by the denoiser as the current z_t. 'z_t' and 'z_{t-1}' are this same slot at consecutive steps, not separate tensors — the two arrows feeding it are two writers at different times, not a sum.
